Our Story

Built on a promise: world-class care, at home.

From a single vision in 2013 to Nigeria's largest aesthetic laser center — this is the Grandville story.

The Beginning

Founded June 1, 2013.

Dr. Aranmolate Rasheed Ayobami founded Grandville Medical and Laser Center with a simple conviction: Nigerians shouldn't have to travel abroad for world-class surgical and aesthetic care.

A burns, plastic and laser surgeon with over 15 years of experience, he built Grandville around one idea — international standards, honest pricing, and results that speak for themselves.

Fourteen years on, that idea has grown into a multiple award-winning healthcare institution with three locations, a trauma center, an ICU, and the largest aesthetic laser center in the country.

Milestones

The road so far.

June 2013

Grandville is founded

Dr. Aranmolate R. Ayobami opens the first Grandville facility in Lagos, offering plastic, laser and general surgical services.

2016

Aesthetic laser center opens

Grandville launches its flagship aesthetic laser center — which grows to become the largest of its kind in Nigeria.

2018

Smile Train partnership

Grandville partners with Smile Train to provide free cleft lip and palate surgeries for children across Nigeria — a commitment that continues today.

2020

Trauma Center & ICU

A 24/7 trauma center opens at Lekki and an intensive care unit at Surulere, bringing emergency and critical care in-house.

2022

Abuja location launches

The third Grandville location opens in Maitama, Abuja — bringing the full spectrum of our services to the FCT.

Today

The best surgical and aesthetic center in Africa

Three locations. Two aftercare recovery homes. 18 specialist services. And a team that still measures success one patient at a time.

What We Stand For

Values that guide every incision.

01 / Clinical Excellence

Evidence over shortcuts

Every procedure follows internationally validated protocols. Our surgeons are fellowship-trained and keep learning — because our patients deserve nothing less than the standard we'd want for our own families.

02 / Radical Honesty

What you need — nothing more

We recommend the procedure that fits your goals, not the one with the highest price tag. If we don't think surgery is right for you, we'll tell you — and point you somewhere that will help.

03 / Access & Responsibility

Care beyond our walls

Through our Smile Train partnership, children born with cleft lip and palate receive free, life-changing surgery. Giving back isn't a campaign — it's part of our operating licence.
