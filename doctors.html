Our Specialists

The team behind the results.

Seven consultant specialists across surgery, anesthesia, urology, orthopedics and radiology — fellowship-trained, internationally connected, and obsessed with outcomes.

Dr. Aranmolate R. Ayobami

Burns, Plastic & Laser Surgeon — Founder

The founder of Grandville Medical and Laser Center. A pioneer of laser liposuction in Nigeria, Dr. Aranmolate has spent 15+ years perfecting aesthetic and reconstructive surgery — from BBLs and tummy tucks to complex burn reconstruction and cleft lip repair with Smile Train.

MBBS · FWACS (Plastic Surgery) · 15+ years experience · Founder, Grandville Medical and Laser Center

Dr. Babatunde Adetunmbi

Consultant Neurosurgeon

A dual-member of the Nigerian Academy of Neurological Surgeons and the American Association of Neurological Surgeons, Dr. Adetunmbi leads our neurosurgery unit — treating brain, spine and nervous-system conditions with 13+ years of surgical precision.

MBBS · FWACS · Nigerian Academy of Neurological Surgeons · American Association of Neurological Surgeons · 13+ years

Dr. Marvina Onosholema Obietan

Consultant Anesthetist & Intensive Care

Dr. Obietan is the calm behind every operation at Grandville. As a consultant anesthetist and intensivist, she oversees anesthesia for all procedures and leads our ICU at Surulere — where vigilance is measured in seconds.

MBBS · PGDA · FMCA · Consultant Anesthetist / Intensive Care

Dr. Taiwo Opeyemi Alabi

Endourologist — Laser Stone & Prostate

Dr. Alabi specializes in endourology with a bias for the laser treatment of kidney stones and prostate conditions. A prolific researcher with 14+ publications, he brings the latest evidence into every laser procedure he performs.

MBBS · FMCS (Urology) · 14+ research publications · Laser stone & prostate specialist

Dr. Sunday Onimisi Salami

Orthopedic Surgeon

An Associate Fellow of the National Faculty of Surgery and a member of the Nigerian Orthopaedic Association, Dr. Salami treats bones, joints and sports injuries with 15+ years of experience — from fractures to complex joint reconstruction.

Associate Fellow, National Faculty of Surgery · Member, Nigerian Orthopaedic Association · 15+ years

Dr. OKOLO Eshemogie

Consultant Anaesthesiologist

Dr. Okolo is a consultant anaesthesiologist who ensures every Grandville procedure begins and ends safely. Trained at the University of Benin, he manages anesthesia across aesthetic, bariatric and general surgery — with a specialty in high-risk cases.

MBBS · PGDA (Uniben) · FMCA · Consultant Anaesthesiologist

Dr. Ayokusibe O. Jaiyeola

Consultant Radiologist

With 17+ years behind the lens, Dr. Jaiyeola heads our diagnostic imaging unit. A member of the Nigerian Medical Association and the Association of Radiologists in Nigeria, her precise reading of scans guides treatment for every Grandville patient.

BSc (Hons) · MBChB · FWACS · Nigerian Medical Association · Association of Radiologists in Nigeria · 17+ years
