About Grandville

Where medical precision meets artistry.

Founded on June 1, 2013 by Dr. Aranmolate Rasheed Ayobami, Grandville began with a single vision: bring international-standard surgical and aesthetic care to Africa — without the medical tourism.

Today, our three locations in Lekki, Surulere and Abuja serve thousands of patients a year, from laser liposuction to bariatric surgery, cleft lip repair with Smile Train, and 24/7 trauma and ICU care.

  • Leonardo laser liposuction & advanced aesthetic surgery
  • Sole Nigerian franchise for DermaFix skincare (50+ products)
  • Smile Train partner — free cleft lip surgeries for children
